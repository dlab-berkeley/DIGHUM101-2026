# DIGHUM101 – Group Project Directions

## Overview

The group project allows you to survey a Python-dependent method in the digital humanities and reflect on its broader contributions to the field. The group project also invites you to think about the collaborative nature of the digital humanities–a field where researchers often work in teams or labs and where the work depends on layers of interdependent technologies and resources. There is no coding required for the group project. Instead, you will be studying related DH papers or projects that use Python to enact the method of your choosing.

The Group Project is worth 40% of your overall grade. It is split into three parts: the Topic Statement, the Short Paper, and the Presentation.

## Topic Statement

The topic statement is a 1-page, single-spaced document that describes the following:

1. The computational method your group intends to survey. This may be a method we discuss in class or something else on approval. Your topic statement must define the method and briefly explain how it’s used in the digital humanities.
2. At least one DH article or project that utilizes the method. This article or project should be introduced as an example of your chosen method. You should provide a brief summary of its subject matter and findings.

__The due date is Friday, June 12th__ at 11:59pm. It is worth __10% of your overall grade.__ You must submit your topic statement as a pdf via BCourse.

Short Paper

The short paper is a roughly 5-page, single-spaced document that covers the following:

1. The computational method your group has studied. Just as you did in the topic statement, you must define the method and briefly explain how it’s used in the digital humanities.
2. Three DH articles or projects that use your method. For each article or project, your paper should:
    - Briefly __summarize__ them
    - __Assess__ them for quality of scholarly work, clarity, and degree of computational complexity
    - __Reflect__ on them. Are they using their method and code to make new knowledge claims about their subject matter? Are they using their method and code to build a new resource or dataset? Are they transparent about their code and data? Were they personally useful or insightful for your own work? Feel free to critique them as necessary.

Your group will need to agree on the DH articles or projects you intend to survey. Your group will also need to delegate tasks for the writing process. __The due date is Friday, July 3rd__ at 11:59pm. __The short paper is worth 20% of your overall grade.__ Each group member must submit your short paper as a pdf on BCourse.

## Group Presentation

At the end of the course, we will have in-class group presentations. These presentations will be 10-12 minutes in length. Using slides and public speaking, your group will discuss your chosen method and the DH articles or projects that you surveyed. The contents of your presentation may repeat or summarize your short paper, but you must deliver the information in ways proper for a presentation format–not simply read your paper. Each group member must take part in the presentation.

__The due date is Wednesday, July 1st__ at 1pm. The group presentation is worth __10% of your overall grade.__ Group presentations will be viewed and graded in class on the last two days of the course.
